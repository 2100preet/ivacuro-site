AI for clinics

AI that does the busywork. The doctor stays in charge.

Every AI feature drafts and suggests. Nothing clinical is final until the doctor approves it, and the AI never gives medical advice.

AI receptionist

Answers phone calls and WhatsApp, and books the slot

The AI receptionist talks to patients in Hindi, English and Hinglish. It recognises returning patients, asks about the problem, how long it has lasted, age and gender, suggests the right doctor and books a real free slot. Then it sends an SMS or WhatsApp confirmation. It can also reschedule, cancel and answer questions about timings, address and fees.

  • Emergency red flags such as chest pain or stroke signs get “call 108 / go to emergency” and are marked URGENT
  • Inbox of call and chat transcripts, plus a callback queue for staff
  • Recording only after the caller agrees, with consent logged
  • Works with Twilio or Exotel phone lines and the WhatsApp Business API. Available now, with setup needed for each clinic
AI intake

The doctor sees the patient's story before they walk in

Every AI-booked appointment comes with an intake card: the complaint, duration and notes from the call. One click copies it into the visit form.

  • One-click prefill of complaints
  • The AI never finalises anything clinical
AI note-taker

An ambient scribe that drafts SOAP notes and the Rx

With the patient's consent, the AI scribe listens to the consultation and drafts Subjective, Objective, Assessment and Plan notes, vitals, diagnosis, advice and suggested medicines. The doctor edits the draft and approves it, and allergy warnings show up on the spot.

  • Drafts only. Nothing is saved until the doctor saves the visit
  • Allergy warnings on suggested medicines
  • AI patient summary: visits, diagnoses, medicines, allergies, abnormal labs and vitals trends in one paragraph

🔔Smart follow-up & no-show nudges

Gentle WhatsApp/SMS reminders for bookings likely to be missed and overdue follow-ups. Sent only with consent.

💬Reports & Rx on WhatsApp

Patients ask on WhatsApp and get their secure portal link with prescriptions and reports, after an identity check.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. IvaCuro runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer. You can also "install" it to the home screen so it opens like an app, with no Play Store needed.

What happens when the internet goes down?

Reception keeps working. New patients, appointments, tokens and status changes are saved on the device and sync automatically when the connection returns. Photo uploads, prescriptions and bills need internet.

Is my patients' data safe? Where is it stored?

Each clinic's data is kept completely separate and protected by role-based access, an audit log and optional 2-step login. Photos are only shown to logged-in staff of your clinic. Even IvaCuro's platform admins cannot open patient records or photos.

Is it compliant with the DPDP Act?

IvaCuro records patient consent purpose by purpose (treatment, photos, WhatsApp reminders, teaching, publicity), keeps the withdrawal history, supports guardian consent for minors and logs every access. These are the building blocks the DPDP Act expects. As the data fiduciary, your clinic remains responsible for how it uses the data.

Which languages does it support?

IvaCuro works in English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali and Gujarati. Each user picks their own language, and each patient's prescription can print in their language. Hindi covers every screen; the other languages cover the most-used screens.

How does the AI receptionist work? Is it safe?

It answers phone calls and WhatsApp messages in Hindi, English and Hinglish, takes the patient's problem, books a free slot and sends a confirmation. It never gives medical advice: red-flag symptoms get “call 108 / go to emergency” and go to the top of the callback queue. Calls are recorded only after the caller agrees. It is available now and needs a phone line (Twilio or Exotel) and WhatsApp Business setup for each clinic, which we help with.

Does the AI note-taker write prescriptions on its own?

No. The AI scribe only drafts SOAP notes and suggested medicines after the patient consents. The doctor edits and approves everything, and nothing is saved until the doctor saves the visit.

Is the teleconsultation compliant?

IvaCuro follows the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020: the patient's consent is recorded, the visit is marked as a teleconsultation, and the e-prescription carries the doctor's registration number. The doctor still decides whether a teleconsult is suitable.

Does it work with ABHA / ABDM?

You can store a patient's ABHA number today. Full ABDM integration (ABHA creation and linking health records) is on our roadmap.

Can I move my existing patient data?

Yes. On paid plans we help import your patient list from Excel or another software. Polyclinic and Hospital plans include help with data migration.

How does billing work? Can I cancel anytime?

Pay monthly or yearly (2 months free) by UPI, card or net-banking. You get a GST invoice. Cancel anytime: you keep read access, and you can export your data.
