Features

Everything a small clinic needs, in one place

Replace the register, the carbon-copy prescription pad, the bill book and the WhatsApp juggling with one easy app for the doctor and the front desk.

Patients & photos

Patient records with clinical photos

Find any patient in seconds by name or mobile number. Take photos with the phone camera and compare before and after side by side, which is ideal for skin, dental and wound care.

  • Complete history: visits, prescriptions, photos and bills on one screen
  • Consent is recorded with every photo, and photos are never stored on the phone
  • Optional ABHA ID field
Visits & prescriptions

Visit notes and clean, printed prescriptions

Record complaints, vitals, diagnosis and advice quickly. Build the prescription with dose, frequency (1-0-1), duration and instructions, then print it on your letterhead or send it on WhatsApp.

  • Vitals: BP, pulse, temperature, weight, SpO₂
  • Follow-up date with reminder
  • Print or save as PDF in one tap
Appointments & tokens

Appointments, walk-in tokens and a live queue

The receptionist books appointments and gives walk-in tokens, and the doctor sees who is waiting. Statuses go from Booked to Arrived to Done, so the waiting room stays calm.

  • Day view with token queue
  • WhatsApp/SMS reminders for tomorrow's patients
  • No-show tracking
GST billing + UPI

GST bills with a UPI QR code

Create a bill in seconds with GST at 0, 5, 12, 18 or 28%, split into CGST and SGST automatically. The patient scans your UPI QR on the receipt, and you see today's cash, UPI and card collection on the dashboard.

  • Sequential bill numbers, discounts and printable receipts
  • Revenue by payment mode

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. IvaCuro runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer. You can also "install" it to the home screen so it opens like an app, with no Play Store needed.

What happens when the internet goes down?

Reception keeps working. New patients, appointments, tokens and status changes are saved on the device and sync automatically when the connection returns. Photo uploads, prescriptions and bills need internet.

Is my patients' data safe? Where is it stored?

Each clinic's data is kept completely separate and protected by role-based access, an audit log and optional 2-step login. Production data is stored on India-region servers with daily backups. Photos are only shown to logged-in staff of your clinic. Even IvaCuro's platform admins cannot open patient records or photos.

Is it compliant with the DPDP Act?

IvaCuro records patient consent purpose by purpose (treatment, photos, WhatsApp reminders, teaching, publicity), keeps the withdrawal history, supports guardian consent for minors and logs every access. These are the building blocks the DPDP Act expects. As the data fiduciary, your clinic remains responsible for how it uses the data.

Can I use it in Hindi?

Yes. Each user can switch between Hindi and English with one tap. More Indian languages are planned.

Does it work with ABHA / ABDM?

You can store a patient's ABHA number today. Full ABDM integration (ABHA creation and linking health records) is on our roadmap.

Can I move my existing patient data?

Yes. On paid plans we help import your patient list from Excel or another software. Polyclinic and Small Hospital plans include data migration.

How does billing work? Can I cancel anytime?

Pay monthly or yearly (2 months free) by UPI, card or net-banking. You get a GST invoice. Cancel anytime: you keep read access, and you can export your data.
